# Battery Materials Pressure Watch

## Supply Signal
Lithium hydroxide refining bottlenecks remain visible in several producer updates.
Contract negotiations suggest elevated processing spreads through the next two quarters.

## Demand Signal
EV and stationary storage deployment continue to grow, but demand growth is uneven by region.
North America projects are gaining momentum while Europe remains slower.

## Strategic Implication
Upstream producers with secured feedstock and processing capacity may hold better pricing power.
Downstream manufacturers could face margin pressure if hedging remains incomplete.
